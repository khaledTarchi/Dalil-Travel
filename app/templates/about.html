Our Mission

OasisStay was born out of a desire to connect travelers with the hidden wonders of the Algerian Sahara. We aim to highlight authentic guesthouses, eco-lodges, and traditional stays located in the most isolated and serene parts of the country.

Authentic Experiences

Our platform focuses exclusively on properties that offer a true connection to the land and its culture. Whether it's a clay castle in Brezina or an eco-camp under the stars in Tassili N'Ajjer, every stay is an experience.

  • Local Cuisine: Enjoy traditional meals prepared by your hosts, ensuring an authentic culinary journey.
  • Remote Locations: Find peace and tranquility away from the bustling city centers.
  • Cultural Exchange: Learn directly from the locals who call the desert home.

For Hosts

We provide a dedicated space for local hosts to share their unique properties with the world. By joining OasisStay, hosts can easily manage their listings, showcase their traditional meals, and connect with travelers seeking genuine experiences.
